## Context

The system kept delivering value, but the cost of change was growing. The proposal was not a rewrite: it was to create boundaries that allowed the existing code to evolve safely.

## Constraints

- External contracts had to remain compatible.
- The team needed to keep shipping throughout the refactor.
- The new architecture had to be verifiable, not merely documented.

## Architecture

Modules were organized around business capabilities. Dependencies point to explicit contracts, and integrations between modules pass through known interfaces. Architecture tests verify boundaries alongside the functional suite.

## What did not work

Moving files into new packages without changing dependencies only created the appearance of modularity. Real progress began when dependency cycles were measured and broken one flow at a time.

## Main lesson

Modularization is not a folder structure. It is the ability to explain **who may depend on whom and why**.
