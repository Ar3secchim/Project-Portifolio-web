## Context

The homelab grew from a desire to understand the complete path of a system. Instead of studying each tool as an island, it makes it possible to follow what happens from network entry to persistence, deployment, and alerting.

## Constraints

- Hardware and power are limited.
- The environment must remain understandable after weeks without changes.
- Personal data, addresses, and secrets never appear in public documentation.

## Architecture

The architecture is organized into four layers: **networking**, **compute**, **storage**, and **platform services**. Each service declares its dependencies, recovery path, and minimum health signals. The goal is not to imitate an entire cloud, but to make distributed systems behavior visible at a scale I can operate.

## What did not work

Automating everything before understanding the flow increased complexity and made simple mistakes harder to locate. The approach changed: first document a manual path, then automate only repeatable and verifiable steps.

## Main lesson

Reliable infrastructure is not infrastructure that never fails. It makes clear **how to detect, contain, and recover** from failure.
