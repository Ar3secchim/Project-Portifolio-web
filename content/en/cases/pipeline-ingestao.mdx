## Context

This case describes the reasoning applied to a professional ingestion flow. Names, business domain, numbers, and infrastructure details were deliberately removed.

## Constraints

- Evolution had to happen without interrupting existing consumers.
- Reprocessing could not duplicate effects.
- Diagnosis should not depend on one person’s context.

## Architecture

The flow began treating reception, validation, transformation, and publishing as observable stages. Each stage records correlation and outcome; invalid inputs move to an explicit treatment path instead of disappearing inside the pipeline.

## What did not work

Generic retries reduced symptoms, but also prolonged permanent failures and made queue behavior harder to understand. The policy was split between transient errors, contract violations, and failures requiring intervention.

## Main lesson

Throughput matters, but predictability is what makes it possible to increase throughput without losing trust.
